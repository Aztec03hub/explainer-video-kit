# Lantern Library in a minute

### Hello @0-6
[warm, reassuring] Lending a book shouldn't mean a spreadsheet and a sticky note. Meet Lantern Library.
> shot: the catalogue, still, full page

### One place @6-14
Every shelf in your branch lives in one place, searchable from the first page.
> shot: slow push in on the search box

### Search @14-22
Type a title, an author, or just a mood. The list narrows as you type.
> shot: cursor moves to the search box

### Cards @22-30
Each card shows the author and how many copies are on the shelf right now. No walking over to check.
> shot: camera on the first card

### Pick one @30-38
Found the one you want? Pick it, and its details slide in beside the list.
> shot: cursor to the Borrow button

### Borrow @38-46
[warm] Now borrow it. One click, and the book is yours for as long as the shelf rule allows.
> shot: click, panel opens

### Details @46-54
The panel shows the due date, the branch, and who else is in line, before you commit.
> shot: camera on the panel

### Confirm @54-62
When it looks right, confirm. That's the whole checkout, with no form to fill in.
> shot: cursor to Confirm loan, click

### Done @62-70
[warm, reassuring] And you're done. A small banner says so, and the book is marked as yours.
> shot: Loan confirmed banner, label

### Reminders @70-78
Three days before it's due, Lantern sends a gentle nudge, so nothing runs late.
> shot: still on confirmed state

### Staff side @78-85
Librarians see every request as it happens, and shelf counts update on their own.
> shot: camera widens

### Try it @85-90
[warm] Lantern Library. Open the catalogue today, and pick your next read.
