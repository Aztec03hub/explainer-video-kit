# Lantern Library in two minutes

## Finding a book

### Hello @0-15
[warm, reassuring] Lending a book shouldn't mean a spreadsheet and a sticky note. Meet Lantern Library, the catalogue your whole branch can search from one page. In about two minutes, you'll see how a loan works from start to finish.
> shot: the catalogue, still, full page

### One place @15-27
Every shelf in your branch lives in one place. Open the page. The whole collection is right there, ready to browse. Nobody has to ask the desk what's on the shelf today.
> shot: slow push in on the search box

### Search @27-39
Type a title or an author, or just a mood. The list narrows as you type, so a long scroll never gets in your way. Readers find their book in seconds.
> shot: cursor moves to the search box

### Cards @39-51
Each card shows the author and how many copies are on the shelf right now. No walking over to check. If a book is out, you know before you leave the sofa.
> shot: camera on the first card

## Borrowing

### Pick one @51-63
Found the one you want? Pick it. Its details slide in beside the list, so you never lose your place in the catalogue. Pick another and the panel simply follows you.
> shot: cursor to the Borrow button

### Borrow @63-74
[warm] Now borrow it. One click, and the book is yours for as long as the shelf rule allows. There's no card to scan and no queue to join.
> shot: click, panel opens

### Details @74-87
The panel lists the due date and the branch. It also shows how many readers are in line, so you know the wait before you commit. Everything you need sits in one place.
> shot: camera on the panel

### Confirm @87-98
When it looks right, confirm. That's the whole checkout, with no form to fill in. [warm, reassuring] A small banner says you're done, and the book is marked as yours.
> shot: cursor to Confirm loan, click, then the banner

## Behind the desk

### Reminders @98-113
Three days before it's due, Lantern sends a gentle nudge. Nothing runs late, and nobody at the desk has to chase a reader. Renewals are one tap away, and returns are just as quick. Busy weeks stay calm.
> shot: still on confirmed state

### Staff side @113-127
Librarians see every request as it happens. Shelf counts update on their own, so the desk always knows what's in. [warm] Lantern Library. Open the catalogue today, and pick your next read from any shelf in the branch.
> shot: camera widens, full page, hold
